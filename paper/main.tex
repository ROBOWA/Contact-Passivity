\section{Proof-of-concept setup}
\label{sec:task}
A planar two-link robot presses a spherical pad against a horizontal surface
while tracking a normal force of $F_n^d=5$~N and a tangential velocity of
$v_t^d=0.05$~m/s. Control runs at 1~kHz, and the nominal surface friction is
$\mu\approx0.30$. Scripted helping and blocking forces act at the end
effector.

The measured interaction is separated into a predicted task-contact wrench,
a model-error residual, and the known human wrench. The controller maintains
separate residual and human energy ledgers and filters the two-dimensional
tangential--normal command when a power or energy bound becomes active. The
human force is assumed known in this study; the objective is to evaluate the
control architecture independently of sensing uncertainty.
